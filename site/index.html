Don't trust the RPC. Check the validators.

A proof bundle carries the signatures Stellar's validators made when they agreed on a ledger, and the hashes that tie that ledger to a transaction or a Soroban contract event. This page checks them against the validators you trust, here in your browser.

The proof is built by an exnode bundle node, which is not trusted: everything it sends is checked here against the validators' signatures. Soroban calls from the last 7 days are proven with their return value and events.

You can also drop a bundle anywhere on this page.

The verifier is externalize-core compiled to WebAssembly, the same code as the externalize CLI. It trusts the public network's tier-1 validators (7 of 10 organizations) or SDF's testnet validators, chosen by the bundle's network.
